1. About these terms

These terms apply to your use of mifos-x-open-banking-relay.pages.dev, which the Mifos Initiative, a Washington State non-profit corporation, runs as part of Mifos X Open Banking. By using the site you agree to them. How we handle data is explained in the privacy policy.

2. What this site does

The site passes your bank's authorisation response back to the Mifos X Open Banking app on your device, and it does nothing else. It is free to use.

The Mifos Initiative is not a bank or a payment service, and this site isn't either. It doesn't hold money, accounts or credentials, it can't start or approve a payment, and it gives no financial advice. Every approval happens at your bank, under your bank's own terms.

3. Testing only

Mifos X Open Banking is a reference app. It currently connects only to the HSBC UK Open Banking sandbox, a test environment that uses test accounts and no real money. Don't rely on the site or the app for real banking.
