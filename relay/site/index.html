Mifos X Open Banking

Consent relay

If your bank sent you here, you can close this tab and go back to the app.

What this site does

When you approve access or a payment at your bank, the bank sends its answer back to a web address registered by the app. Banks that use the OpenID hybrid flow put that answer in the part of the address after the #, which only your browser can read. This page reads it and hands it straight to the Mifos X Open Banking app on your device.

  1. You approve the request at your bank.
  2. Your bank redirects your browser to /callback/ on this site.
  3. The page passes the answer to the app on your computer, or opens the app on your phone.

What happens to your data

  • Nothing is stored. There is no server code, database, cookie or analytics here.
  • The bank's answer is removed from your browser history as soon as it has been read.
  • The site loads no third-party code, cannot be embedded in another page, and is never cached.
  • It holds no keys or credentials, so it cannot sign in or make payments on its own.
