Open Banking relay
Privacy Terms Source

Your bank's answer, passed straight back to the app.

If your bank sent you here, you're done. You can close this tab and go back to Mifos X Open Banking.

  1. You approve at your bankYou sign in on your bank's own site and approve access or a payment.
  2. Your bank sends you hereThe bank's answer arrives in the part of this page's address that only your browser can read.
  3. The app picks it upThis page hands the answer to the app on your computer, or opens the app on your phone.

What happens to your data

  • Nothing is stored. This site has no server code, database, account, cookie or analytics.
  • The bank's answer never reaches our host. It sits after the # in the address, which browsers keep to themselves.
  • The answer is removed from your browser history as soon as it has been read.
  • No third-party code is loaded, the site can't be embedded in another page, and nothing is cached.
  • The site holds no keys or credentials, so it can't sign in to your bank or make payments.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
